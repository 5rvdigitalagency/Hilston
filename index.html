The Hilston Park
Experience

Hilston Park is a countryside escape in Wales offering residential stays and memorable experiences within a private estate surrounded by woodland and open landscapes.

Welcoming schools, corporate teams, families, and private groups for over 50 years, the estate combines adventure, learning, relaxation, and shared experiences in one scenic location with a wide range of outdoor activities for adults and groups.

Discover what Hilston Park offers:

  • 15+ guided outdoor group activities
  • Residential stays range from dormitories to private manor accommodation
  • Flexible spaces for retreats, events, and team experiences
  • Outdoor experiences for schools, adults, and private groups
  • On-site catering with adaptable meal arrangements

Hilston Park Outdoor Centre hosts school residential trips, outdoor learning programmes, corporate retreats, and private group stays throughout the year.

Hilston Park is a charity-run residential centre managed by an experienced team delivering programmes for schools, corporate groups, and private visitors.

The earliest recorded building on the estate dates to 1293, giving Hilston Park a history spanning more than 700 years.

Hilston Park welcomes school groups, corporate teams, families, and community organisations, with stays tailored to suit different group requirements.

Residential stays include accommodation, on-site catering, and access to activities, with arrangements customised to meet each group’s needs.
