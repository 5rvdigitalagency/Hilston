The Hilston Park
Experience

Hilston Park is a countryside escape in Wales offering residential stays and memorable experiences within a private estate surrounded by woodland and open landscapes. Welcoming schools, corporate teams, families, and private groups for over 50 years, the estate combines adventure, learning, relaxation, and shared experiences in one scenic location with a wide range of outdoor activities for adults and groups.

Discover what Hilston Park offers:

  • 15+ guided outdoor group activities
  • Residential stays range from dormitories to private manor accommodation
  • Flexible spaces for retreats, events, and team experiences
  • Outdoor experiences for schools, adults, and private groups
  • On-site catering with adaptable meal arrangements

Hilston Park: Backed by 50+ Years of Safety, Trust & Expertise

Hilston Park combines over 50 years of experience with carefully managed stays, outdoor learning, and guest support across the estate.

AALA-licensed activities with clear safety procedures designed for schools, youth groups, and organised stays.

Prepared meal arrangements designed around allergy, dietary, and medical requirements shared before arrival.

Outdoor activities are matched to age, group ability, and experience levels for safe and enjoyable participation.

Quiet spaces and flexible programmes that help guests feel comfortable, included, and supported throughout visits.
