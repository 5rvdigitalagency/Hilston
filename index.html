The Hilston Park
Experience

Far from the busier towns of South Wales, Hilston Park sits within acres of open meadows in the Welsh countryside. Featuring a 19th-century Palladian mansion between the River Wye and the Black Mountains, the estate carries a quietness that deeply supports well-being.

Serving as an outdoor activity centre in Wales for over 50 years, the estate has welcomed schools, corporate teams, and families to encourage personal-level development.

Discover what Hilston Park offers:

  • Historic grounds within the Welsh countryside
  • Residential stays, from dormitory accommodation to full manor use
  • 15+ outdoor group activities and guided experiences
  • Flexible spaces for learning, team development, and private events
  • On-site catering with adaptable meal arrangements

Residential Stays & Accommodation

The heritage of Hilston Park is shown in its rooms. With a choice of historic Manor House settings or private countryside accommodation, we provide flexible, 1 to 7+ night residential trips for schools, corporate groups, and private visitors.

Exclusive large-scale residential accommodation. Ideal for schools, large corporate groups, and full-site bookings.

Hilston Park: Estate Managed with 50+ Years of Safety and Expertise

Hilston Park runs on care, planning, and a deep understanding of how people grow. A glimpse of what guests can expect during their time here:

An AALA-licensed environment with established safety measures for all school and youth groups, supported by clear on-site procedures.

Quiet spaces and adaptable programmes designed to help guests settle, take part, and feel comfortable throughout their stay.

Allergy and medical needs shared in advance, with meals prepared accordingly and reviewed before arrival.

The Gatehouse and Courtyard Rooms offering self-contained stays for families, corporate groups, and private bookings.

Over 15 outdoor activities designed for ages 8+, with sessions matched to group ability and setting.

An experienced team on-site supporting group leaders with timings, activities, and daily logistics.
