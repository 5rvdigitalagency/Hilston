The Hilston Park
Experience

Far from the busier towns of South Wales, Hilston Park sits within acres of open meadows in the Welsh countryside. Featuring a 19th-century Palladian mansion between the River Wye and the Black Mountains, the estate carries a quietness that deeply supports well-being.

Serving as an outdoor activity centre in Wales for over 50 years, the estate has welcomed schools, corporate teams, and families to encourage personal-level development. Groups come to find focus in a scenic woodland valley, portions of which are home to rare dragonflies and native plant species.

At Hilston Park, the environment provides the true meaning of outdoor learning and residential stays within a single, unified estate.

Discover what Hilston Park offers:

  • Historic grounds within the Welsh countryside
  • Residential stays, from dormitory accommodation to full manor use
  • 15+ outdoor group activities and guided experiences
  • Flexible spaces for learning, team development and private events
  • On-site catering with adaptable meal arrangements

Hilston Park is the Estate Managed
with 50+ Years of Safety and Expertise

Hilston Park runs on care, planning, and a deep understanding of how people grow.
A glimpse of what guests can expect during their time here:

All staff hold current DBS certificates and follow comprehensive safeguarding policies approved by relevant authorities.

Our catering team accommodates all dietary requirements, allergies and preferences with advance notice.

All programmes are designed and risk-assessed for the specific age group and ability level of each group.

A dedicated coordinator is available throughout your visit to ensure everything runs smoothly.
