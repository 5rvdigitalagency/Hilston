Ways to Reach Hilston Park

Hilston Park, situated between the River Wye and the Black Mountains, is well-connected by road, rail and air. Multiple travel options are available to suit all journeys.

By Car

Hilston Park sits beside the B4347 road in the Monnow Valley, approximately 8 miles northwest of Monmouth and just over a mile southwest of Skenfrith. The approach roads pass through open meadows, riverside woodland and quiet country lanes, so the drive itself is worth taking slowly.

Driving Directions

  • From the South (Newport/Cardiff): Take the A449 north to Raglan, then follow the B4233 toward Abergavenny. Pick up the B4347 north toward Skenfrith. Hilston Park is signposted on the left before reaching the village.
  • From the North (Hereford): Head south on the A465, then turn off toward Skenfrith via the B4521. Follow the B4347 southward. The park entrance is approximately one mile past Skenfrith.
  • From the East (Monmouth): Take the B4233 west from Monmouth town centre and join the B4347 heading northwest. Hilston Park is roughly 8 miles from Monmouth.

By Air: Fly In, Then Journey Through the Valley

The nearest major international airports are Cardiff, Bristol and Birmingham. All of them offer scheduled flights from across Europe and beyond.

Nearest Airports

  • Cardiff Airport (CWL) is approximately 45 miles away, about 1 hour by car.
  • Bristol Airport (BRS) is approximately 60 miles; around 1 hour 15 minutes by car.
  • Birmingham Airport (BHX) is approximately 75 miles away, about 1 hour and 30 minutes by car.

Public Transport: The Straightforward Route

The nearest train station is Abergavenny, approximately 12 miles from Hilston Park. From there, local bus services run toward Monmouth, from where a taxi or arranged transfer can complete the journey to the park.

Local Transport Options

  • Nearest Train Station: Abergavenny, approximately 12 miles away, with regular services from Cardiff, Newport and Birmingham.
  • Bus Services: Routes 43 and 60 connect Abergavenny to Monmouth. Check current timetables before travel.
  • Taxi or Transfer: Available from both Abergavenny and Monmouth for the final leg into the park.
