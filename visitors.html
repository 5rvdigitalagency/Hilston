Visitor Guide

Everything you need to plan a smooth and memorable stay at Hilston Park, from daytime activities to overnight accommodation and event planning support.

Plan Your Stay

Choose the format that suits your group: educational trips, private events, or family accommodation in the Welsh countryside.

  • School and youth group experiences
  • Corporate and private events

Before You Arrive

For group organisers and guests, we recommend confirming schedules, dietary needs, and room allocation details before arrival.

  • Confirm attendee numbers and timings in advance
  • Share dietary requirements with the team early
  • Prepare practical clothing for outdoor activities
  • Use the contact page for planning assistance

Need direct planning support? Use the booking button or contact the Hilston Park team.
