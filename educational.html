Progressive Educational Trips for Schools

Learning beyond the school gates is the moment that teaches more than it excites.

At Hilston Park, students learn through safely structured outdoor experiences that leave a lasting impact. A day programme or a residential stay in the Welsh countryside provides access to outdoor education activities that foster personal development for primary to Post-16 pupils. With over 50 years of experience, trained staff, and inclusive SEND support, Hilston Park ensures the safety of every child within larger groups.

Educational Programmes by Age Group

Every programme the staff at Hilston Park designs matches the age, confidence and readiness of the students group.

KS2 (Ages 8–11) — Primary School Residential Away from home for the first time. We make sure it feels like the right decision.
KS3 (Ages 11–14) — Secondary Outdoor Learning The age where curiosity kicks in. We give them the space to explore it safely.
KS4 (Ages 14–16) — GCSE Age Group Ready for more. These sessions give them the challenge to match it.
Post-16 & College Groups Last year of education. These sessions prepare for what comes next.
SEND & Inclusion Groups Every student participates. Our staff adapt pace, group size and setting for every need.

School Group Accommodation at Hilston Park

For large group residentials of 30 or more, Hilston Park’s historic manor house gives schools a fully self-contained space where students sleep, eat and spend time with groups.

Sleeping Arrangements

Hilston Park offers bunk room accommodation for large school groups, with staff rooms nearby to keep supervisors close to students.

Dining Hall

Students can eat together in Hilston Park’s heritage dining hall, where every meal is freshly prepared on-site.

Shared Spaces

The manor house has shared common areas where students can relax, socialise and wind down between activity sessions, all within the building and within sight of staff.

Activity Access

Every activity area is within walking distance on the estate. No coaches, no off-site travel, no wasted time in the day.
