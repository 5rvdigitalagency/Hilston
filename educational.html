Progressive Educational Trips for Schools

Learning beyond the school gates is the moment that teaches more than it excites.

At Hilston Park, students learn through safely structured outdoor experiences that leave a lasting impact. A day programme or a residential stay in the Welsh countryside provides access to outdoor education activities that foster personal development for primary to Post-16 pupils.

From collaborative problem-solving tasks to outdoor challenges, students learn the importance of teamwork and communication in a safe environment.

With over 50 years of hosting flexible educational stays, Hilston Park ensures the safety of every child within larger groups. Our trained staff also support children with Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND), so they fully experience the rewards of outdoor learning.

School Group Accommodation at Hilston Park

For large group residentials of 30 or more, Hilston Park’s historic manor house gives schools a fully self-contained space where students sleep, eat and spend time together.

Sleeping Arrangements

  • 16 bunk rooms sleeping 130+ students
  • Additional beds available on the lower floor
  • Staff rooms within the same building, keeping your team close to students at all times
  • Room allocation organised by class or year group, confirmed before arrival

Students develop confidence in a new environment, safe independence with staff always nearby, and collaborative group skills through shared living.

Dining Hall

Students can eat together in Hilston Park’s heritage dining hall, where every meal is freshly prepared on-site.

Shared Spaces

The manor house has shared common areas where students can relax, socialise and wind down between activity sessions, all within the building and within sight of staff.

Activity Access

Every activity area is within walking distance on the estate. No coaches, no off-site travel, no wasted time in the day.
