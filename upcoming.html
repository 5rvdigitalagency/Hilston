GROWING FOR THE FUTURE

A New Chapter in the Hilston Park Experience

There's plenty happening behind the scenes at Hilston Park. We're investing in exciting new facilities and outdoor experiences designed to bring even more opportunities for adventure, learning and personal development.

From woodland treetop challenges to new river activities, discover the developments we're working towards for 2027 and follow our journey as they take shape.

LOOKING AHEAD TO 2027

Discover What's Coming Soon

Four exciting developments that will bring new challenges, experiences and opportunities to Hilston Park.

01
COMING IN 2027 OUTDOOR ADVENTURE

High Ropes Course

Seven obstacles. One exciting new challenge.

We're developing a brand-new 7-obstacle linear high ropes course, giving participants the opportunity to take on exciting aerial challenges and push beyond their comfort zones.

Designed to encourage personal growth, the course will help individuals build confidence, resilience and the ability to work together as a team.

Confidence Resilience Teamwork Problem-solving Achievement
02
COMING IN 2027 WOODLAND ADVENTURE

Clip and Go Treetop Adventure

Discover the woodland from a whole new perspective.

An exciting new Clip and Go treetop adventure is planned for Hilston Park. Participants will navigate a series of elevated challenges through the woodland.

The experience will culminate in three zip lines, offering an exciting finish and unique views of the surrounding countryside.

Adventure Exploration Confidence Coordination Teamwork
03
PLANNED FOR 2027 WATERSPORTS & EXPLORATION

River Activities – River Monnow

New adventures on the water.

We're expanding our watersports provision with planned canoeing and river-running activities on the River Monnow.

Proposed routes from Skenfrith towards Monmouth will provide opportunities to explore the surrounding landscape while developing water-based skills, environmental awareness and teamwork.

Outdoor adventure Water skills Exploration Environment Teamwork
04
COMING IN 2027 SKILL & PRECISION

GB Standard Archery Range

A new space to develop focus and precision.

We're introducing a new GB-standard archery facility to provide a professional environment for beginners and more experienced participants.

Whether learning the fundamentals or refining existing skills, archery offers an opportunity to develop concentration, discipline, precision and confidence.

Concentration Focus Discipline Precision Confidence
