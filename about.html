About
Hilston Park

Between the Black Mountains and the River Wye, Hilston Park sets its historic heritage across meadows and woodlands. Serving as an outdoor activity centre in Monmouth for over 50 years, the estate has supported meaningful residential experiences for groups.

For students, days here bring space to learn together, build confidence, and take part in shared residential experiences. For corporate teams, it offers a space to refresh, reconnect, and return with better focus. For families and teams, it provides a calm environment where connecting with nature feels more present and meaningful.

Accommodation, activities, and catering are all in one place, so visitors can settle in easily and have a memorable experience. Each stay is supported by experienced, DBS-checked staff with clear safeguarding procedures. SEN considerations are also supported through a flexible and inclusive approach to ensure all guests can take part comfortably.

Our History

Hilston Park has a long and established history within the Monmouthshire countryside. The written records of the estate trace back to the 13th century, where it began as a manor estate near Skenfrith. Over centuries, the land evolved under the care of prominent local families and became a heritage estate with unique surroundings.

13th Century

The Origins

The written records of the Hilston estate trace back to 1293, where it began as a manor estate near Skenfrith in Monmouthshire. Over centuries, the land evolved under the care of prominent local families and became a heritage estate shaped by the Welsh landscape.

1838

The Palladian Rebuild

The original house was damaged by fire and later rebuilt in a Palladian style. The new building still stands today as a Grade II listed estate, known for its simple, balanced architecture and prominent position in the Monmouthshire countryside.

Mid-1900s

A Changing Estate

Over the years, ownership of the estate changed several times, with each period shaping its character further. By the mid-1900s, its use began to shift from a private home toward a more open educational purpose, setting the stage for what Hilston Park would become.

1971

An Education Centre is Born

By 1971, Hilston Park officially became an outdoor education centre. School groups began arriving for residential stays, adventure activities, and learning beyond the traditional classroom — the beginning of 50+ years of meaningful group experiences.

Today

Hilston Park Today

Hilston Park continues as a leisure centre in Monmouth, hosting schools, corporate groups, and private visitors. With accommodation for over 130 guests, in-house catering, and more than 20 outdoor activities, the estate remains shaped by the same values of learning, wellbeing, and shared experience that have guided it for over 50 years.

Safety Standards
and Guidelines

DBS-Checked Staff

All staff working with groups at Hilston Park are DBS-checked through the Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS). This ensures that everyone involved in delivering residential programmes has been appropriately vetted to work with children and vulnerable individuals.

Safeguarding Procedures

Proper safeguarding procedures are in place across all residential stays and are followed consistently by the team. The procedures guide supervision, conduct, and response protocols, ensuring a safe and well-managed environment for schools, corporate groups, and private visitors.

Risk Assessments

Risk assessments are completed for activities and residential stays to support safe delivery across all programmes. These are used to plan group activities responsibly and are available to assist schools and organisers with their internal approval processes where required.

Duty of Care

A clear duty of care is maintained throughout every stay at Hilston Park. Experienced staff oversee visitors from arrival to departure, ensuring that accommodation, activities, and site use are managed safely and appropriately at all times.
