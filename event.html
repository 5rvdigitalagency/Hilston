Outdoor Team Building Events Set to Challenge, Connect and Energise

Most teams arrive at Hilston Park not quite sure what to expect. By the end of the day, we ensure that hesitation is removed.

Our outdoor team building activities put people in situations they would never encounter in the office. Someone who is usually quiet becomes the one leading the group. A team that barely talks outside meetings starts laughing. The Welsh countryside does something to people that a conference room cannot replicate.

We plan the entire experience, including activities, catering, and accommodation if needed. You share your group size, your goals, and your dates. We build the day around you.

Whether you are organising a one-day session or a multi-day residential retreat, Hilston Park provides the space, activities, and support needed for a genuinely stress-free group experience.

What’s Included in Your Event Experience

Hilston Park gives groups access to meet, stay, eat, and take part in outdoor activities within one countryside venue. A highlight of our services:

  1. On-Site Residential Accommodation

    Groups staying for more than one day can use the Manor House and Courtyard Rooms. For day visits and short-term events, indoor meeting spaces and outdoor areas are made available.

  2. Outdoor Team Building Activities

    The estate includes more than 15 outdoor activities used for leadership retreats, team building, problem-solving exercises, and group challenges. Activities can be added to both day events and residential programmes.

  3. Planned Catering

    Meals are included in your event. We serve group meals at agreed-upon times and handle all dietary requirements in advance, including vegetarian, vegan, and allergies, so your schedule stays uninterrupted.

  4. Meeting Spaces & Outdoor Areas

    We provide your group with dedicated indoor rooms for meetings, workshops, and briefings, with outdoor space available for team activities and breakout sessions.
