Every Event Finds Its Setting at Hilston Park

From weddings and family celebrations to corporate retreats and outdoor team building events, Hilston Park provides a unique countryside setting for memorable and meaningful group experiences.

The historic manor house, private estate grounds, and Wye Valley surroundings create the perfect backdrop for celebrations, workshops, retreats, and team activities, all within one location. We tailor every event around your needs, combining accommodation, catering, activities, and event spaces to deliver seamless one-day gatherings or multi-day residential experiences.

Stay on the Estate —
Accommodation at Hilston Park

For groups staying overnight, Hilston Park offers two distinct accommodation options within the estate, keeping your entire event — activities, dining, and sleeping — in one place.

Manor House

The main residential building on the estate is suited to large group events and multi-day corporate residentials.

  • Sleeps 130+ guests across 16 rooms
  • Additional beds available on the lower floor
  • Unique, shared dining experience with active staff
  • All activity and event spaces are within walking distance on the estate

Courtyard Rooms

Private, restored rooms within the estate grounds, suited to smaller corporate stays, families, and special occasions.

  • Private individual rooms with rich experience
  • Suited to groups of 2 to 6
  • Direct access to the grounds and activity areas
  • Better suited for guests who need their own space throughout the stay
