Every Events Set to Challenge, Connect and Energise

Most teams arrive at Hilston Park not quite sure what to expect. By the end of the day, we ensure that hesitation is removed.

Our outdoor team building activities put people in situations they would never encounter in the office. Someone who is usually quiet becomes the one leading the group. The Welsh countryside does something to people that a conference room cannot replicate. We plan the entire experience, including activities, catering, and accommodation if needed. You share your group size, your goals, and your dates. We build the day around you.

Event Hosting Spaces and Residentials

For groups booking an event or residential stay, Hilston Park offers two accommodation options on site:

Manor House

The main residential building on the estate is suited to large group events and multi-day corporate residentials.

  • Sleeps 130+ guests across 16 rooms
  • Additional beds available on the lower floor
  • Unique, shared dining experience with active staff
  • All activity and event spaces are within walking distance on the estate

Courtyard Rooms

Private, restored rooms within the estate grounds, suited to smaller corporate stays, families, and special occasions.

  • Private individual rooms with rich experience
  • Suited to groups of 2 to 6
  • Direct access to the grounds and activity areas
  • Better suited for guests who need their own space throughout the stay
