Choose a Different Way to Spend a Weekend

The moment you arrive at Hilston Park with your family or friends, the pace changes. Far from the tourist hordes, the estate sits across 60 acres of Victorian land, setting a different rhythm for those who value privacy and open countryside. Children notice the space first, stepping out into the fresh air where the weekend feels different.

This holiday accommodation in Mid Wales brings groups together rather than splitting them into separate routines. With self-catered cottages and full-board options, the setting is built for shared meals and Wye Valley walks. Here, the noise of daily life is replaced by simple time together, making it easy to stay present and enjoy the Welsh landscape.

Estate Accommodation

Where You’ll Stay

Three distinct spaces across the Hilston Park estate — each offering a different character for families, private groups, and holiday stays in Wales.

01

Manor House

The principal residence of the estate, built for communal gathering. The Manor House is the heart of the Hilston Park experience for large groups.

  • Large capacity across multiple rooms
  • Shared living and dining spaces
  • Suitable for organised residential stays
  • Often used for multi-family and extended group breaks

Best suited for guests who want a shared, communal experience on the estate.

02

Courtyard Rooms

A more private and considered accommodation option, set within the working courtyard of the estate.

  • Private rooms for smaller groups
  • Ideal for short breaks and weekend stays
  • Suitable for families or couples
  • Direct access to estate grounds and activities

Perfect for guests who prefer a relaxed and more private setting.

03

The Gatehouse

A self-contained private residence at the entrance to the estate. The Gatehouse offers house-style living within the grounds.

  • Private house-style accommodation
  • Multiple bedrooms for family or group use
  • Separate living and bathroom facilities
  • Suitable for family holidays and private stays

Ideal for guests who want their own space within the Hilston Park estate.
