Accommodation at Hilston Park Wales: A Unique Setting for Group Stays and Corporate Events

Finding the right place for a group to stay in Wales is about more than having enough beds. The setting, facilities, atmosphere and things to do can all make a real difference to the experience.

Finding the right place for a group to stay in Wales is about more than having enough beds. The setting, facilities, atmosphere and things to do can all make a real difference to the experience.

For schools, families, organisations and businesses, Hilston Park accommodation offers something a little different: a historic estate surrounded by countryside, with space for groups to stay, spend time together and enjoy outdoor experiences.

Set near Monmouth in South Wales, Hilston Park sits between the Black Mountains and the River Wye. Its 50+ acre estate of meadows and woodland provides a peaceful backdrop for residential stays, group activities and events.

Whether you're planning a team away day, a company retreat, a family gathering or a group residential, accommodation at Hilston Park Wales provides a countryside alternative to the usual hotel stay.

Why Choose Hilston Park Accommodation?

When you're organising a group trip, keeping everyone together can make life a lot easier.

Instead of booking individual hotel rooms and arranging activities at different locations, staying at a dedicated group venue allows everyone to share the same base.

Hilston Park accommodation is designed around residential group experiences, giving visitors a place to stay while also providing access to outdoor spaces and activities.

The historic surroundings add to the character of the experience, whilst the countryside setting offers plenty of opportunities to step away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

A Countryside Escape Near Monmouth

Location can make or break a group getaway.

Hilston Park is situated near Newcastle, Monmouth, in Gwent, South Wales, between the Black Mountains and the River Wye.

The surrounding countryside makes it possible to enjoy a quieter pace without giving up the convenience of being near Monmouth.

For businesses, this can create a welcome change from the office. For families and groups, it provides an opportunity to spend quality time together in a natural setting.

And let's be honest, sometimes getting everyone away from their phones and into the fresh air is half the battle.

  • Corporate teams
  • Schools and educational groups

  • Private groups

  • Organised residential groups

Hilston Park's Historic Setting

Hilston Park isn't a modern purpose-built conference centre. Its character comes from centuries of history.

The estate dates to the 13th century, with origins recorded from 1293.

Following fire damage, the house was rebuilt in 1838 in the Palladian style and is now a Grade II-listed building.

In 1971, Hilston Park became an outdoor education centre.

Today, the historic estate continues to welcome groups, combining its heritage with residential accommodation, outdoor activities, catering, and group experiences.

A leadership retreat?
