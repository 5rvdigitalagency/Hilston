Choose a Different Way to Spend a Weekend

The moment you arrive with your family or friends at Hilston Park, the pace changes. Far from the tourist trails, the estate sits across 60 acres of Victorian land, setting a different rhythm for those who value privacy and open countryside. Children notice the space first, stepping out into the fresh air where the weekend feels different.

This holiday accommodation in Mid Wales brings groups together rather than splitting them into separate routines. With self-catered cottages and full-board options, the setting is built for shared meals and Wye Valley walks. Here, the noise of daily life is replaced by simple time together, making it easy to stay present and enjoy the Welsh landscape.
