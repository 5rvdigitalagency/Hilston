A Stay at Hilston Park Can Teach You More Than You Think

Life at home, school and work often follows familiar routines. A stay at Hilston Park gives people a chance to do things differently. Far from the tourist hordes, the estate sits across 60 acres of Victorian land, setting a different rhythm for those who value privacy and open countryside.

Living and spending time together brings its own lessons. People learn to work as a team, take responsibility, respect different needs and make decisions together. At Hilston, children can help with shared tasks, look after their belongings and learn to consider other people. Families and friends can share meals, enjoy the grounds and make time for conversations that busy schedules often leave behind.

Accommodation for Every Group

Hilston Park Accommodation Options

The Hilston Park estate has comfortable, high-quality spaces for every visiting group. Explore our stay options below to see what suits your trip.

01 — For School Groups

Student Accommodation

Shared dormitory-style accommodation helps pupils build friendships with classmates and develop everyday life skills.

  • Bunk beds in shared dormitory rooms
  • Separate facilities for boys and girls
  • Staff rooms nearby for supervision
  • Shared indoor spaces for group use

The experience: Living with classmates, building friendships, working together, and maintaining personal routines.
The positives: Independence, teamwork, responsibility and learning to compromise.

Max: 130 guests
Type: Dormitory / Bunk beds
02 — For Teachers

Teacher Accommodation

With dedicated staff rooms, teachers can rest after a busy day with their pupils. Communal spaces are also available to enjoy with colleagues.

  • En-suite rooms on the ground floor
  • Communal spaces for relaxation
  • Close to the pupils’ living areas
  • Breakfast and meal arrangements can be discussed

The experience: Time to wind down, share a conversation with colleagues or enjoy a hot chocolate beside the fire in the Manor House foyer.
The positives: Comfort, rest and time to socialise with colleagues.

Max: Confirm with the Hilston Park team
Type: Staff rooms
03 — For Families

Family Accommodation

Stay and experience the heritage of Hilston Park with your family. We have accommodation options suited to family breaks and weekend getaways.

  • Family rooms with en-suite facilities
  • Sleeping arrangements for adults and children
  • Shared spaces for time together
  • Close to the estate grounds and activities area

The experience: Spend the day outdoors, share meals and return to your room to relax together after a day of activities.
The positives: Quality family time, shared experiences and a break from everyday routines.

Max: 4 guests
Type: Family room / En-suite
04 — For Couples and Individuals

Private or Couple Accommodation

Private rooms in the Manor House offer couples and individual guests a comfortable place to stay, with views of the estate grounds in selected rooms.

  • King and double-bed options
  • En-suite or private bathroom, depending on the room
  • Garden or grounds views in selected rooms
  • Access to shared estate spaces

The experience: Wake up to views of the countryside grounds and enjoy quality time near the lake area and woodlands.
The positives: Privacy, quality time and a comfortable base for a countryside break.

Max: Depends on the room
Type: Private room / King or double bed
05 — For Work Retreats

Corporate Group Accommodation

Group accommodation at Hilston Park gives colleagues a place to stay together during residential meetings or work retreats.

  • Multiple bedrooms for group stays
  • Shared living and dining spaces
  • Classroom and meeting spaces
  • Outdoor activities available by arrangement

The experience: Spend the day in meetings or team activities, share meals with colleagues and continue conversations in the evening without having to travel elsewhere.
The positives: Time to work together away from the usual workplace, build relationships and share ideas.

Max: Confirm with the Hilston Park team
Type: Group accommodation / Shared spaces
06 — For Events and Celebrations

Accommodation for Events and Celebrations

Whether you need the full Manor House or an outdoor space for your event, Hilston Park offers an elegant setting for celebrations and private gatherings.

  • Multiple bedrooms for overnight stays
  • Shared living and dining spaces
  • Outdoor ground space for events
  • Catering and event support, subject to arrangements

The experience: Host your event, enjoy special moments with your guests, stay overnight and make use of the space and facilities available.
The positives: Event space and overnight accommodation in one location, with less need to arrange separate places to stay.

Max: Confirm for the selected booking
Type: Manor House / Outdoor event space
07 — For Small Groups

Small Group Accommodation

Groups of 5–10 people can choose between private Courtyard Rooms and the self-contained Gatehouse, depending on their needs and availability.

  • Private rooms in the Courtyard Rooms
  • Gatehouse with a private entrance
  • Separate living space in the Gatehouse
  • Access to the estate grounds

The experience: Spend the day enjoying the estate, then return to your own rooms or relax together in the Gatehouse living space.
The positives: A choice of private rooms or self-contained accommodation, with space to relax after a day out.

Max: 5–10 guests, depending on accommodation
Type: Private rooms / Self-contained accommodation

Hilston Park Accommodation Options

Choose the accommodation setting that best suits your group, from shared manor house stays to private rooms and self-contained spaces.

Manor House

The main accommodation for large group stays.

  • Large-capacity accommodation across multiple rooms
  • Shared living and dining spaces for group use
  • Suitable for organised group stays and residential trips
  • Often used for multi-family holidays and extended group breaks

Best suited for guests who want a shared experience during their stay.

Courtyard Rooms

A more private and comfortable accommodation option within the estate.

  • Private rooms for smaller groups
  • Ideal for short breaks and weekend stays
  • Suitable for families or couples
  • Close access to estate grounds and activities

Perfect for guests who want a more relaxed and private experience.

Gatehouse

A private accommodation space for families and small groups.

  • Private house-style accommodation
  • Multiple bedrooms for family or group use
  • Separate living and bathroom facilities
  • Suitable for family holidays and private group stays

Best suited for guests who want their own space within the estate.
