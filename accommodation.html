Choose a Different Way to Spend a Weekend

Escape the pace of everyday life at Hilston Park, where families and friends can enjoy peaceful holiday accommodation in Mid Wales set within 60 acres of countryside. With space to relax, explore, and spend time together, it offers a refreshing alternative to busy tourist destinations.

  • Set within 60 acres of historic estate grounds
  • Ideal for families, friends, and private groups
  • Self-catered and full-board stay options available
  • Perfect for shared meals, countryside walks, and quality time together

What’s Included in Your Private & Family Stay

For private stays and family groups, Hilston Park offers a welcoming and self-contained setting where guests can unwind, share meals, and enjoy time together in the heart of the countryside.

Sleeping Arrangements

A mix of flexible room options makes it easy for families and groups to stay together, with simple, comfortable spaces suited to relaxed group breaks.

Dining Hall

The on-site dining space provides a central place to gather, whether for self-catered meals or organised dining during your stay.

Shared Spaces

A range of communal areas throughout the house give guests plenty of room to sit back, spend time together, and enjoy a slower pace.

Activity Access

The surrounding grounds and outdoor spaces are just a short walk away, giving guests the freedom to explore and enjoy the setting at their own pace.

Activities Included with Accommodation in the Wye Valley

From peaceful walks across the estate to engaging outdoor challenges, our staff-led programmes create a refreshing digital detox in the UK, helping guests reconnect with nature and each other in a calm countryside setting.

Estate Walks & Nature Trails

Explore woodland paths, meadows, and estate grounds through guided walks that showcase local wildlife, landscapes, and countryside surroundings.

Archery & Target Sports

Enjoy supervised archery and target-based activities suitable for families, private groups, and guests seeking outdoor challenges.

Kayaking & Outdoor Water Activities

Take part in water-based activities within the Wye Valley, enjoying the region’s natural scenery and outdoor environment.

Campfire & Outdoor Cooking

Relax with evening campfire sessions and outdoor cooking experiences, ideal for residential groups and shared outdoor moments.
