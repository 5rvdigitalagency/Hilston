At Hilston Park, every activity is as safe as it is challenging. Whether climbing at height or navigating water-based tasks, participants are equipped with the right gear and guided through every step.

What every session includes:

  • Professional supervision throughout, from briefing to finish
  • Activities built to boost teamwork, leadership and personal confidence
  • Complete access for schools, corporate groups, families and residential guests
  • Flexible scheduling (subject to weather conditions and staff availability)
  • Suitable for ages 8 and above, with selected activities available for younger children on request
PLAN YOUR GROUP EXPERIENCE

Plan Your Group Experiences By Activity Type

From high ropes to water activities and bushcraft, every session is guided by qualified instructors and adapted to your group.

1

High Ropes & Team Challenges

Jacob’s Ladder

A climbing challenge where groups must rely on teamwork, balance, and communication to help each other reach the top.

Leap of Faith

Participants climb to a height before taking a controlled jump designed to build confidence and trust through personal challenge.

Gladiator Challenge

Competitive outdoor tasks that encourage teamwork, quick decision-making, and communication in a fun and active setting.

High & Low Ropes Courses

Obstacle-based rope courses designed to improve confidence, focus, coordination, and group communication at different challenge levels.

Balance Beam

A simple activity that tests balance, concentration, and teamwork while encouraging groups to support each other throughout the challenge.

Climbing

Instructor-led climbing sessions that help participants build confidence, determination, and problem-solving skills through outdoor challenge experiences.

2

Water-Based Activities

Canoeing

A guided water activity where groups paddle together while improving teamwork, coordination, and communication on the water.

Paddle Boarding

A relaxed but active water experience focused on balance, confidence, and enjoying outdoor activity in a supervised environment.

Raft Building

Teams work together to build and test their own raft using shared planning, communication, and problem-solving skills.

3

Outdoor Learning & Survival Activities

Bushcraft Skills

Hands-on outdoor sessions teaching practical survival skills, teamwork, and confidence in natural outdoor surroundings.

Rope Rescue

A technical outdoor challenge focused on communication, teamwork, and controlled rescue exercises supervised by trained instructors.

Mountaineering

Outdoor walking and climbing experiences designed to encourage resilience, teamwork, and confidence in changing terrain.

Night Hikes

Guided evening walks that encourage awareness, communication, and teamwork while exploring outdoor environments after dark.

Designated Camping Areas

Outdoor camping spaces designed for shared group experiences and quality time spent in natural surroundings.

4

Group Bonding Experiences

Campfire Experiences

Relaxed evening gatherings where groups can unwind, share stories, and spend quality time together after outdoor activities.

FAQ

Frequently
Asked
Questions

Activities are included in your booking, not as an add-on. What you pay depends on your group size, how long you are staying and what you want to do. The easiest way to work it out is to get in touch, and we will go from there.

Most of our activities are open to ages 8 and above. If you are bringing younger children, some newer activities can be arranged for them too. Just let us know at the time of booking, and we will check what is available for your group.

We adapt around the group. If a student needs a slower pace, a smaller group or a quieter setting, we work around that. Schools usually bring their own SEN staff, and we work alongside them. No student should feel left out, and we do our best to ensure that does not happen.

The activities we offer are not only available to corporate groups but also to educational trips, families, and private stays. From high ropes and bushcraft to water activities and team challenges, everything is included in your stay at Hilston Park.

Every activity within Hilston Park is run by qualified instructors who brief the group before anything starts. The right equipment is provided, and someone always stays with the group.
